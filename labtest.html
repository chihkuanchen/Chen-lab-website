Our Research

We combine computational methods with biological insights to understand how pathogens evolve and spread.

🦠 Viral Evolution

Studying the evolutionary dynamics of RNA viruses, with particular focus on influenza and emerging pathogens. Our computational models reveal how viruses adapt to host immunity over time.

🌳 Phylogenetics

Developing advanced phylogenetic methods to reconstruct evolutionary trees from sequence data. We apply these techniques to track pathogen transmission chains and emergence events.

📊 Epidemiology

Using computational models to predict outbreak trajectories and evaluate intervention strategies. Our work informs public health decision-making during epidemic situations.

🔬 Protein Structure

Analyzing viral protein structures to understand how mutations affect immune recognition and drug resistance. We use structural biology to predict viral evolution patterns.

💻 Computational Tools

Creating open-source software for sequence analysis, phylogenetic inference, and data visualization. Our tools are widely used in research and public health institutions worldwide.

🌐 Real-time Tracking

Maintaining interactive platforms that track pathogen evolution in real-time. Our dashboards provide immediate insights into viral dynamics during active outbreaks.

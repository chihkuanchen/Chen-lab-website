About Chen Lab

Using birds as a major model, we ask how cell types and gene regulatory networks evolve to generate tissue diversity. Combining single cell transcriptomics along with epigenomics tools, our research focuses on three major directions: Evo-Devo of Skin Appendages, Regulatory Innovation, and Tissue Anti-aging.

我們以鳥類為主要模式生物，探討細胞類型與基因調控網絡如何演化，並進一步塑造組織的多樣性。藉由整合單細胞轉錄體學與表觀基因體學技術，我們聚焦於三大研究方向：皮膚附屬器官的演化與發育、基因調控創新，以及組織抗老化。

Research

Evo-Devo of Skin Appendages

Using birds as a model, we aim to identify the molecular mechanisms underlying the evolutionary development of diverse skin appendages, with a specific focus on avian-specific tissues.

我們以鳥類作為研究模式，致力於解析多樣化皮膚附屬器官演化發育的分子機制，並著重探討鳥類特有組織的形成與演化。

Regulatory Innovation

Uncovering how novel regulatory elements, including alternative isoforms and transposon-derived sequences, contribute to the emergence of new cell states and morphologies.

探討新穎調控元件，包括選擇性轉錄異構體（alternative isoforms）以及源自轉座子的序列，如何促成新細胞狀態與形態的產生。

Tissue Anti-aging

Characterizing gene regulatory differences in aging tissues between humans and non-model species. Recognizing the limitations of mice as models for aging research, we aim to leverage biological diversity to develop new strategies for understanding and potentially mitigating human tissue aging.

比較人類與非模式物種老化組織中的基因調控差異。考量到小鼠作為老化研究模式的侷限性，我們希望善用生物多樣性，發展新的研究策略，以深入了解人類組織老化的機制，並探索延緩組織老化的潛在途徑。

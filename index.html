About Chen Lab

Using birds as a major model, we ask how cell types and gene regulatory networks evolve to generate tissue diversity. Combining single cell transcriptomics along with epigenomics, we study the gene regulatory elements and networks that drive cell type identity and tissue-specific developmental programs.

🔬

Research

Tissue Homology

Investigating fundamental molecular processes including DNA replication, transcription, and protein synthesis. Our work contributes to understanding how genes are regulated during development and how cell types are conserved across species.

Regulatory Innovation

Developing advanced genetic tools and techniques for precise DNA manipulation. We explore applications in agriculture, medicine, and biotechnology with a focus on understanding evolutionary innovation in gene regulation.

Cellular senescence

Studying cellular structures, functions, and interactions. Our research examines cellular signaling, metabolism, and disease mechanisms at the molecular level, with emphasis on how regulatory networks evolve.

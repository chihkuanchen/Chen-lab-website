About Chen Lab

Using multi-omics approaches to decipher the evolution of gene regulation across tissues

Chen Lab is dedicated to understanding how gene regulatory networks evolve to generate phenotypic diversity across species and tissues. We employ cutting-edge multi-omics technologies including genomics, transcriptomics, chromatin accessibility, and epigenetics to uncover the molecular mechanisms underlying regulatory evolution.

Our research focuses on several key areas: regulatory sequence evolution, tissue-specific gene expression patterns, evolutionary conservation of regulatory elements, and the role of gene regulation in adaptation. We collaborate with researchers worldwide to advance our understanding of evolutionary biology and gene regulation.

We are committed to training the next generation of computational and molecular biologists. Our lab values rigorous science, collaborative spirit, and innovative thinking.

Our Research

We investigate fundamental questions about how gene regulation evolves

Regulatory Evolution

Understanding how promoters, enhancers, and other regulatory elements evolve across species and contribute to phenotypic diversity.

Tissue-Specific Gene Expression

Investigating how tissue-specific regulatory networks develop and maintain distinct gene expression patterns while preserving core cellular functions.

Comparative Genomics

Using comparative approaches across multiple species to identify conserved regulatory principles and discover novel regulatory mechanisms.

Chromatin Organization

Studying how 3D chromatin structure influences gene regulation and how these structures evolve across different species.

Epigenetic Regulation

Examining histone modifications, DNA methylation, and other epigenetic marks as key regulators of evolutionary change.

Adaptation & Evolution

Linking regulatory changes to organismal adaptation and investigating the molecular basis of evolutionary innovations.

About Chen Lab

Dedicated to advancing scientific knowledge through innovative research and collaboration

Chen Lab is committed to conducting high-quality research that pushes the boundaries of biological science. With state-of-the-art equipment and a talented team of researchers, we tackle challenging problems in molecular biology and genetics.

We believe in the power of collaboration and open science. Our research findings are published in leading scientific journals and presented at international conferences, contributing to the global scientific community.

Research Focus Areas

We pursue research across multiple disciplines to understand biological systems

🧪

Molecular Biology

Investigating fundamental molecular processes including DNA replication, transcription, and protein synthesis. Our work contributes to understanding how genes are regulated and expressed.

🔬

Genetic Engineering

Developing advanced genetic tools and techniques for precise DNA manipulation. We explore applications in agriculture, medicine, and biotechnology.

🦠

Cell Biology

Studying cellular structures, functions, and interactions. Our research examines cellular signaling, metabolism, and disease mechanisms at the molecular level.

🧬

Genomics

Analyzing whole-genome sequences to understand genetic diversity and evolution. We use bioinformatic approaches to identify novel genetic factors in disease.

💊

Biomedical Research

Developing therapeutic approaches for human disease. Our translational research bridges basic science with clinical applications.

🌿

Synthetic Biology

Designing and engineering biological systems for novel functions. We create synthetic organisms and systems for environmental and industrial applications.
